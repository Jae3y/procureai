# Introduction

Welcome to Kora!

In this documentation, you’ll find comprehensive information on how to successfully integrate our services with your web and mobile applications. It's developer-friendly and we've filled it with helpful examples, but if you have any questions, you can head over to our [Help Center](https://support.korapay.com) to get more information or better, [contact our Support Team](mailto:support@korapay.com).

Kora offers everything you'll need to create delightful payment experiences for your customers. Whether you're building a simple platform to collect payments from your customers or creating a powerful application to manage your business payments, we will provide you with all the resources needed to achieve your payment goals.
