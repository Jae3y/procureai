# KYB Verification (eIDV) in Nigeria

Kora's Identity Service enables businesses to verify government-issued business documents in Nigeria. This includes:

* [Certificate of Incorporation (CAC)](https://developers.korapay.com/docs/nigeria-certificate-incorporation)

> 💡
>
> It is important to always have sufficient funds in your available balance to successfully verify identities.
