# Bank Account Verification

Bank account verification enables you to verify that a bank account exists and retrieve the account holder's details. This is available for Nigerian and Kenyan bank accounts.

* [Nigerian Bank Account Verification](https://developers.korapay.com/docs/nigerian-bank-account-verification)
* [Kenyan Bank Account Verification](https://developers.korapay.com/docs/kenyan-bank-account-verification)
