# Security & Compliance  

* Voucher codes and PINs are highly sensitive data.
* Do not log full voucher codes or PINs in your application logs or monitoring tools.
* Ensure your integration complies with Kora’s security and data protection standards as outlined in your Merchant Service Agreement.
